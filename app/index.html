    Why nothing else is flagged

    • Every trophy-related quest giver stays available after the story in free-roam, even those who go quiet for a while mid-story (Kenton is the example).
    • Nothing on the 100% Completion checklist is missable, and the checklist only opens after the story anyway.
    • Optional missions do disappear over time, and some fences and magazine spots are lost for good — but none of that feeds any trophy or the 100% checklist.
    • There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual save slots) as insurance.
